# TENDER NOTICE

## 1. Eligibility — Registered Company

The bidder must be a legally registered construction company in the Republic of Kenya, with a valid company registration certificate. Foreign bidders must register under the Companies Act and demonstrate local partnership with a Kenyan-registered firm.

## 2. Mandatory — NCA Registration

The bidder must hold a valid National Construction Authority (NCA) registration in Road Works Category A (Road Category 1 — projects above KES 100M) or Category B (Road Category 2 — KES 30M-100M). The NCA certificate must be current as of the tender submission deadline.

## 3. Mandatory — ISO 9001 Certification

The bidder must submit a valid ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System certificate. The certificate must be issued by an accredited certification body and must be current as of the tender submission deadline.

## 4. Compliance — Tax Compliance Certificate

The bidder must submit a valid Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) issued by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA). The TCC must be current as of the tender submission deadline.

## 5. Technical — Prior Experience

The bidder must demonstrate prior experience in road rehabilitation projects of comparable scope (≥ KES 60M) within the last five (5) years. The bidder must submit at least two (2) completion certificates from previous county or KeRRA clients.

## 6. Financial — Quote Completeness

The bidder must submit a complete financial quote, itemised by BoQ: (i) earthworks, (ii) sub-base and base course, (iii) asphalt concrete surfacing, (iv) drainage, (v) road furniture, (vi) traffic management. The quote must include VAT and must be valid for 120 days.

## 7. Technical — Project Methodology

The bidder must submit a project methodology, including a Gantt chart, a quality assurance plan, and a health, safety, and environment (HSE) plan compliant with the Occupational Safety and Health Act 2007. The methodology must demonstrate a clear understanding of Mombasa's coastal climate (corrosion, drainage) and urban traffic management.

## 8. Eligibility — Audited Accounts

The bidder must submit audited financial statements for the last three (3) financial years, audited by a practising CPA (K) registered with ICPAK.

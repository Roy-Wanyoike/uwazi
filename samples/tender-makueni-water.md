# TENDER NOTICE

## 1. Eligibility — Registered Company

The bidder must be a legally registered company in the Republic of Kenya, with a valid company registration certificate from the Business Registration Service (BRS). Bidders registered in other East African Community (EAC) partner states are eligible under the EAC Common Market Protocol, provided they submit a recognised equivalent registration.

## 2. Mandatory — ISO 9001 Certification

The bidder must submit a valid ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System certificate. The certificate must be issued by an accredited certification body and must be current as of the tender submission deadline. Bids without this certificate will be declared non-responsive.

## 3. Technical — Prior Experience

The bidder must demonstrate prior experience in water reticulation projects of comparable scope (≥ KES 15,000,000) within the last five (5) years. The bidder must submit at least two (2) completion certificates from previous clients as evidence.

## 4. Compliance — Tax Compliance Certificate

The bidder must submit a valid Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) issued by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA). The TCC must be current as of the tender submission deadline. Bids without a valid TCC will be declared non-responsive per the Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act, 2015.

## 5. Financial — Quote Completeness

The bidder must submit a complete financial quote, itemised by BoQ (Bill of Quantities). The quote must include all applicable taxes (VAT, withholding tax) and must be valid for 120 days from the tender submission deadline.

## 6. Technical — Project Methodology

The bidder must submit a project methodology, including a Gantt chart, a quality assurance plan, and a health, safety, and environment (HSE) plan. The methodology must demonstrate a clear understanding of the project scope and the local context of Makueni County.

## 7. Eligibility — Audited Accounts

The bidder must submit audited financial statements for the last three (3) financial years. The statements must be audited by a practising CPA (K) registered with ICPAK.

## 8. Evaluation Weights

The total evaluation weight is 100%, distributed as follows: Technical (45%), Financial (35%), Mandatory Documents (10%), Eligibility (10%). A bid that fails to meet any mandatory criterion will be declared non-responsive and will not proceed to the scoring stage.

# TENDER NOTICE

## 1. Eligibility — Registered Company

The bidder must be a legally registered company in the Republic of Kenya, with a valid company registration certificate. Bidders registered in other East African Community (EAC) partner states are eligible under the EAC Common Market Protocol.

## 2. Mandatory — EPRA License

The bidder must hold a valid Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority (EPRA) license as an Electrical Contractor Category A (large installations). The license must be current as of the tender submission deadline. Bids without this license will be declared non-responsive.

## 3. Mandatory — ISO 9001 Certification

The bidder must submit a valid ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System certificate. The certificate must be issued by an accredited certification body and must be current as of the tender submission deadline.

## 4. Compliance — Tax Compliance Certificate

The bidder must submit a valid Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) issued by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA). The TCC must be current as of the tender submission deadline.

## 5. Technical — Prior Experience

The bidder must demonstrate prior experience in solar street lighting projects of comparable scope (≥ KES 20M) within the last four (4) years. The bidder must submit at least two (2) completion certificates from previous county or KETRACO clients.

## 6. Financial — Quote Completeness

The bidder must submit a complete financial quote, itemised by component: (i) solar panels (×400), (ii) LED luminaires (×400), (iii) lithium batteries (×400), (iv) galvanised poles (×400), (v) civil works, (vi) installation, (vii) commissioning. The quote must include VAT and must be valid for 120 days.

## 7. Technical — Project Methodology

The bidder must submit a project methodology including a Gantt chart, a quality assurance plan, and a commissioning protocol. The methodology must demonstrate site selection (high-traffic junctions, market centres), pole foundation design (Nakuru's volcanic soil), and grid-synchronisation where applicable.

## 8. Technical — Warranty

The bidder must provide a minimum 5-year warranty on solar panels and LED luminaires, a 3-year warranty on lithium batteries, and a 10-year structural warranty on galvanised poles against corrosion.

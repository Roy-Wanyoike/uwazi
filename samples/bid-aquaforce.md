We propose a four-phase delivery over 24 weeks. Phase 1 (weeks 1-3): mobilisation and site survey. Phase 2 (weeks 4-14): pipe laying and storage tank construction. Phase 3 (weeks 15-20): pump installation and testing. Phase 4 (weeks 21-24): commissioning, training, and handover. A full Gantt chart is enclosed as Annex E. The quality assurance plan (Annex F) provides for weekly inspections by the County Water Engineer. The HSE plan (Annex G) provides for PPE for all site workers, daily toolbox talks, and an on-site first-aid responder.

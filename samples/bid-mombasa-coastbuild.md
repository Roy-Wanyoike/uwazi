## Project Methodology

We propose a four-phase delivery over 32 weeks. Phase 1 (weeks 1-3): site mobilisation + traffic study. Phase 2 (weeks 4-22): earthworks + drainage. Phase 3 (weeks 14-28): sub-base + asphalt surfacing (overlapping Phase 2). Phase 4 (weeks 29-32): road furniture + commissioning. The coastal-climate adaptation plan uses chloride-resistant asphalt mix + elevated drainage culverts (Annex F). The HSE plan provides for PPE, daily toolbox talks, and on-site first-aid responder (Annex G).

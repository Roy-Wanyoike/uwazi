Our itemised financial quote, including VAT, is: (i) Solar panels 100W (×400) @ KES 4,800 = KES 1,920,000; (ii) LED luminaires 60W (×400) @ KES 12,500 = KES 5,000,000; (iii) Lithium batteries 100Ah (×400) @ KES 18,500 = KES 7,400,000; (iv) Galvanised poles 8m (×400) @ KES 22,500 = KES 9,000,000; (v) Civil works — KES 4,200,000; (vi) Installation — KES 3,800,000; (vii) Commissioning — KES 1,200,000; Subtotal: KES 32,520,000; VAT (16%) — KES 5,203,200; Contingency — KES 676,800. Total: KES 38,400,000. Quote valid for 150 days.

## Project Methodology

We propose a four-phase delivery over 24 weeks. Phase 1 (weeks 1-3): site survey + soil testing (volcanic-ash soil in Nakuru requires reinforced base). Phase 2 (weeks 4-18): pole fabrication + installation. Phase 3 (weeks 8-20): solar panel + battery mounting (overlapping Phase 2). Phase 4 (weeks 21-24): commissioning + handover. Gantt chart enclosed as Annex F. QA plan covers panel-output testing per IEC 61215 and IP-65 luminaires. The commissioning protocol is enclosed as Annex G.

## Warranty

We provide a 7-year warranty on solar panels (output ≥ 85% at year 7), 5-year warranty on LED luminaires, 4-year warranty on lithium batteries (cycle life ≥ 2,000), and 12-year structural warranty on galvanised poles against corrosion. The warranty is enclosed as Annex H.

# TENDER NOTICE

## 1. Eligibility — Registered Company

The bidder must be a legally registered company in the Republic of Kenya, with a valid company registration certificate from the Business Registration Service (BRS). Bidders registered in other East African Community (EAC) partner states are eligible under the EAC Common Market Protocol.

## 2. Mandatory — ISO 13485 Certification

The bidder must submit a valid ISO 13485:2016 Medical Devices Quality Management System certificate. The certificate must be issued by an accredited certification body and must be current as of the tender submission deadline. Bids without this certificate will be declared non-responsive.

## 3. Compliance — KEBS Certification

The bidder must submit a Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) certification or letter of no objection for the medical equipment being supplied. Equipment must meet Kenya's medical device importation requirements under the Pharmacy and Poisons Act.

## 4. Compliance — Tax Compliance Certificate

The bidder must submit a valid Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) issued by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA). The TCC must be current as of the tender submission deadline. Bids without a valid TCC will be declared non-responsive.

## 5. Technical — Prior Experience

The bidder must demonstrate prior experience supplying medical equipment to a public health facility in the last five (5) years. The bidder must submit at least two (2) completion certificates from previous hospital or county health department clients as evidence.

## 6. Financial — Quote Completeness

The bidder must submit a complete financial quote itemised by equipment category (patient monitors, anesthesia machines, ICU beds, theatre equipment). The quote must include all applicable taxes and must be valid for 120 days from the tender submission deadline.

## 7. Technical — Delivery Timeline

The bidder must submit a delivery timeline not exceeding 90 days from the date of the Local Purchase Order (LPO). The timeline must include installation, commissioning, and user training at each of the three county hospitals (Mama Lucy, Pumwani, and Mbagathi).

## 8. Technical — Warranty

The bidder must provide a minimum 24-month warranty on all supplied equipment, including on-site repair, preventive maintenance every 6 months, and availability of spare parts for a minimum of 7 years from commissioning.

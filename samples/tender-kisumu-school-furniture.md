# TENDER NOTICE

## 1. Eligibility — Registered Company

The bidder must be a legally registered company in the Republic of Kenya, with a valid company registration certificate. Cooperatives and women/youth-owned enterprises are encouraged to apply under the AGPO (Access to Government Procurement Opportunities) reservation.

## 2. Compliance — KEBS Certification

The bidder must submit a Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) certification or quality mark for the furniture being supplied. The furniture must meet KS 234:2018 (Kenya Standard for school furniture — dimensions, materials, and ergonomics).

## 3. Compliance — Tax Compliance Certificate

The bidder must submit a valid Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) issued by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA). The TCC must be current as of the tender submission deadline.

## 4. Technical — Prior Experience

The bidder must demonstrate prior experience supplying school furniture (desks, chairs, tables) to a public school, county education department, or the Ministry of Education in the last three (3) years. The bidder must submit at least one (1) completion certificate.

## 5. Financial — Quote Completeness

The bidder must submit a complete financial quote, itemised by furniture type: (i) two-seater desks (×800), (ii) pupil chairs (×1600), (iii) teacher tables (×120), (iv) classroom storage cabinets (×120). The quote must include VAT and must be valid for 90 days.

## 6. Technical — Delivery Timeline

The bidder must submit a delivery timeline not exceeding 60 days from the date of the LPO. The timeline must include delivery to 40 primary schools across Kisumu Central, Kisumu East, and Kisumu West sub-counties.

## 7. Technical — Quality Assurance

The bidder must submit a quality assurance plan covering (a) raw material inspection, (b) in-process inspection during manufacture, (c) final pre-delivery inspection, and (d) post-delivery inspection in the presence of head teachers.
